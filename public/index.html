03 / Not a strawman

A serious objection belongs in the room.

James Woodall: Art Vs. Slop — Can AI Make Art?

Woodall's public video essay is a significant critical reference for this project. His discussion invites a distinction between the experience of making something and simply encountering an output; it also raises concerns about human creative practice and the structure of AI production.

Watch Woodall's original essay ↗

Independent study and response. Woodall is not presented as a collaborator, sponsor, or endorsing reviewer. We have not verified an academic professorship.

What the postcard cannot settle

Someone may value a picture deeply. That does not prove its makers, source contributors, or affected workers were treated fairly. Equally, a concern about labor or provenance cannot by itself describe every viewer's experience of that picture.

The next question is not “Which side won?” It is “Which claim has evidence, who is missing from the account, and what would change our minds?”
